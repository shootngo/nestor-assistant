# Phases

Phase 1 is the only implemented work. Phases 2–7 are stubs so the sequence stays visible; do not start them until Phase 1 is confirmed on the fridge tablet.

## 1. Tablet shell — **this repo, now**

Expo Android kiosk placeholder: landscape, keep-awake, immersive-ish system UI, sideload APK. No voice, no cloud, no dashboard.

## 2. Wake / sleep word

Porcupine (or equivalent) always-listening wake and sleep phrases. Not started.

## 3. Speech in / speech out

Android SpeechRecognizer for capture and TTS for Nestor’s replies. Not started.

## 4. Nestor brain

Connect the tablet to the household assistant backend. On-screen identity stays **Nestor**; do not name the model. Not started.

## 5. Household data

Firestore / existing Nestor PWA data: shopping, calendar, and related household state. Not started.

## 6. Kitchen dashboard

Cards, egg face / idle animation, news and weather loop. Not started.

## 7. Overnight presence

Dim / ambient overnight mode. Not started.
